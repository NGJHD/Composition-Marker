Copy the page out VERBATIM. That means:

- Keep every spelling mistake, every wrong tense, every missing or wrong
  punctuation mark exactly as the child wrote it. You are not correcting
  anything. A single corrected word makes the marking wrong, because the child
  will be told they wrote something they did not write.
- Keep the paragraph breaks. Start a new line where the child started a new

  paragraphs together.

  off the end of a ruled line, join it up.
- If there is a title or a heading at the top of the page, put it on its own
  first line.
- If a word has been crossed out, leave it out. If a word has been inserted
  above the line with a caret, put it where the caret points.
- If you genuinely cannot read a word, write [?] in its place. If you can make
  a good guess, write your guess followed by [?] with no space, like this:
  bicyle[?]. Do not guess at a whole sentence.
- Do not add page numbers, do not add your own notes, and do not describe the
  photograph.
