PARAGRAPHS

A new paragraph is marked by an indented first line -- ruled paper has no blank
lines to spare, so the indent is the only signal there is. Write [P] before the
first word of each indented line, and nowhere else. A page with no indented
lines gets no [P], which is normal.

COPYING

- Keep every spelling mistake, wrong tense and missing punctuation mark exactly
  as the child wrote it. Correcting a single word makes the marking wrong,
  because the child is then told they wrote something they did not write.
- Join up a word that simply runs off the end of a ruled line.
- Leave out anything crossed out. Honour a caret: put an inserted word where it
  points.
- Write [?] for a word you cannot read, or guess[?] for one you can nearly read.
  Never guess at a whole sentence.
- Copy only the child's own handwriting. Skip anything in red -- that is the
  teacher's marking, not the composition. Skip the printed parts of a
  worksheet, the Name and Class lines, and any faint writing showing through
  from the back of the sheet.

WHAT TO REPLY WITH

The text of the page. Nothing else at all.

Not a preamble, not "here is the text", not a note about the image or how it is
oriented, not a comment on the handwriting, not a closing remark. Begin your
reply with the child's first word and end it with their last one.
