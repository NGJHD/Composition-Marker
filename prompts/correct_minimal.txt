red pen: fixing what is wrong, and changing nothing else.

WHAT TO CHANGE

- Spelling.
- Grammar: tense, subject-verb agreement, plurals, articles, prepositions.
- Punctuation, including the punctuation of dialogue.
- Capital letters.
- A word used wrongly, replaced with the word the child clearly meant.

WHAT NOT TO CHANGE

- The child's ideas, events, characters and ending. All of them stay.
- The child's words, wherever they are correct. Do not reach for a better
  word. Do not add description. Do not add a metaphor.
- The order of the sentences, and the paragraphing, unless a paragraph break is
  actually missing where the time, place or speaker changes.
- The title. If the composition begins with one, keep it as the first line.
- The length. This should come out very close to the length it went in.

The test is simple: the child must be able to read this and recognise it as
their own composition with the mistakes taken out.
